# Competitor research

Regenerate `competitor-research.md` at the repo root by researching real
competitors on the web.

## Steps

1. **Find 3 to 4 real, comparably small shops** via `WebSearch` — independent
   art-print or print-on-demand businesses of a similar scale to Canvas & Co
   (a two-person studio), not large marketplaces (Etsy, Society6, Minted,
   Redbubble) or enterprise print labs. Favor shops with a clear "about" page
   or distinctive marketing copy you can quote or paraphrase.
2. **Confirm each one with `WebFetch`** on its own site (about/home page)
   where possible, to get a specific, sourced observation rather than relying
   on search snippets alone. If a site blocks fetching (403), fall back to
   the WebSearch summary but still cite the URL.
3. **Write one line per shop**: what it emphasizes in its *own* marketing —
   a distinctive phrase, claim, or angle (e.g. limited-edition scarcity,
   national retail reach, a founder story) — with the shop name and URL.
   Only use specific, sourced observations; never invent or assume a claim a
   shop hasn't actually made.
4. **Identify one gap**: something a small, handmade, two-person studio like
   Canvas & Co can credibly claim that these particular competitors can't,
   based directly on what step 3 found (e.g. they've scaled into wholesale/
   retail distribution, sell scarce collector editions, use an outside
   printer, or lead with a different tone/audience than "everyday home").
   Don't reach for a generic "we're more authentic" claim — ground it in the
   specific contrast just researched.
5. **Write `competitor-research.md`** at the repo root (overwrite if it
   exists): a short list of the shops (name, URL, one-line emphasis) followed
   by a short "gap" paragraph. Keep the **entire file under 150 words** —
   count before finishing and cut rather than pad.

## Constraints

- Every claim about a competitor must trace to something you actually read
  on their site or in a search result — no invented positioning.
- Stay under 150 words total; this is a scan for internal use, not a report.
