# Canvas & Co Design

Build the Canvas & Co site with a warm, handmade, gallery-like feel: calm and unhurried, never corporate or hypey.

## Colors

| Role | Value |
|------|-------|
| Background (warm cream) | `#fbfaf7` |
| Cards | `#ffffff` |
| Text (dark ink) | `#20242c` |
| Secondary text (muted gray) | `#6f7480` |
| Accent (terracotta) | `#b5673f` |
| Borders (hairline) | `#ece8e1` |

## Layout

- Keep content centered, about 1080px wide.
- Use generous spacing and lots of breathing room.

## Components

- Rounded cards, 12 to 14px radius, with thin 1px hairline borders (`#ece8e1`).
- Pill-shaped tags.

## Typography

- Big, calm headings.
- System sans-serif.
- Roomy line height, around 1.55.

## Content

- Show prices in whole US dollars (e.g. `$55`, not `$55.00`).
