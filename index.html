About the studio

Canvas & Co is a two-person art-print studio in Portland, Oregon. Every piece is drawn in-house and printed on premium canvas, so it arrives ready for a nail and a wall.

Most of our neighbors and customers are settling into a new home or looking for a thoughtful gift. We hope you find something that feels like yours.
